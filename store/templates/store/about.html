FOURTR // ABOUT

FOURTR operates at the intersection of dark minimalism and modern street culture. Built for the architectural purist, we prioritize heavy-gauge raw materials, structural silhouette, and restraint.

LIMITED RUN ARCHITECTURE

We reject fast fashion and mass production. Every piece released under the FOURTR mark is produced in strictly controlled batch quantities. Once a drop sells out, it enters our permanent archive and will not be reproduced in the same exact specification.

CRAFT & WEIGHT

Custom heavyweight organic cottons, reinforced construction, and industrial hardware designed to withstand long-term wear.

LOCAL DISPATCH

Designed and curated in Nigeria, serving fashion-forward individuals across Africa with streamlined digital verification.
